What can be checked in a contractor estimate?

Contractor estimates can contain dozens of quantities, prices, allowances, fees, exclusions, and project terms. Second Check It provides an automated second pass over the document before you approve it.

Things the scan looks for

  • Arithmetic, subtotal, or total inconsistencies
  • Potentially duplicated or overlapping charges
  • Conflicting quantities, prices, dates, or descriptions
  • Missing information that appears important to the estimate
  • Fees, exclusions, assumptions, or limitations worth noticing
  • Vague or internally inconsistent wording

What it does not do

Second Check It does not determine whether a contractor's price is fair, verify market pricing, inspect workmanship, or provide legal or construction advice. Automated analysis can also miss issues or flag something that turns out not to be a problem.

Use the results as questions to investigate before making a significant decision.
